Frequently Asked Questions

What makes your dual Marketer & Developer approach unique?

+
Most agencies suffer from a communication divide: developers build websites without understanding conversion tracking or SEO schema, while marketers launch ads without being able to fix Core Web Vitals or implement custom API funnels. Waseem Abbas unifies both disciplines into a single high-velocity execution engine.

How soon can we launch our campaigns or web development?

+
Campaign audits and initial paid media setups typically launch within 3 to 5 business days. Full-stack custom web applications and e-commerce platforms typically deploy in agile sprints between 2 to 4 weeks depending on architectural scope.

Do you work on fixed-price projects or monthly retainers?

+
Both models are supported. Web engineering builds and landing page overhauls can be structured as fixed-price milestones, while continuous paid media management and technical SEO scaling operate on monthly performance retainers.

What tracking and analytics stack do you implement?

Every project is equipped with server-side Facebook Conversions API (CAPI), Google Analytics 4 via Google Tag Manager, custom offline event conversion tracking, and session recording tools like Hotjar to guarantee accurate data attribution.
